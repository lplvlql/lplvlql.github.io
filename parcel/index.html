parcel

↓

A desktop file-sharing app built on Ledger. Point it at a folder and it serves verified bytes to anyone with the link.

In development.

How it works

Scans a folder, serves it, and joins peers from invites or ledger:// links. Everything it fetches is addressed by content, so what arrives is what was asked for. Ships as Parcel.app.

Parcel Pocket

The phone companion to Parcel. Same invites, same verified bytes, from your pocket.
