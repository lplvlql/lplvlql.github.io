One-table Flash Poker: you plus three local bots on a persistent portrait felt. Boots straight to the table — no login, no lobbies, no shop. Bust, knock, run it back.

How a hand plays

  • Four fresh seats every hand, one suit each, dealt into a single shared 52-card deck.
  • Every seat antes 1. No blinds. Simultaneous concealed opens, then call or fold.
  • Staged chips stay reversible until the knock seals them. A card flick folds.
  • Board cards can rewrite the rankings mid-hand — a flush suddenly outranking a full house applies to everyone at once.
  • Best five of seven takes the pot. Broke seats get a bust chip and walk back in all-in.

The table

Burgundy felt, blackened metal, brass. Monochrome cards, pixel opponents, a bell, a deck lift, and a knock pad. Phone-first, portrait, one screen.

Play it
