Usual Time

Mono delay, Loop/Sample memory, and immediate DIRECT effects for Daisy Terrarium. The Mac audition app shares the DSP, so what you hear on the desk is what the pedal runs.

HardwareDaisy Terrarium
EffectsDelay · Loop/Sample · DIRECT
CompanionMac audition app
CodeLocal project

Documentation

  • Specification: every band, mode/switch interaction, musical design, and constraints.
  • Manual: controls and gestures.
  • Architecture: modules, algorithms, settings, and ownership.
  • Testing: validation commands, acceptance gates, and evidence requirements.

Build

Firmware builds with make all; the Mac app with make -C app all. Recipe selection matters — plain defaults do not necessarily reproduce an archived image. Retained prototypes are indexed in the algorithm garden.

Status

Active firmware + app project. Deployment snapshots, qualification gaps, and the next experiment live in the project TODO. This site is the public summary; full docs ship with the source.
